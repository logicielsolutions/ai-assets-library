# Brand: Logiciel

## Colours
- Background: paper `#FAF8F5`. Light backgrounds only.
- Text: ink `#0F0F10`, charcoal `#444545`
- Panels: sand tones
- Red `#A41E21`: only on the logo and one small dot. No red text, no red plates.

## Type
- Display: Satoshi 500 (free, fontshare.com)
- Body: Inter
- Labels: JetBrains Mono, caps
- Keep type small and quiet: headline about 13% of frame height, a short stacked phrase in the empty part of the scene.
- One headline or one subtitle on screen, never both.

## Logo
- Get the current logo files from marketing and put them in `brand/`.

## Imagery
- Generated images carry the human story: people at work, paperwork piling up, a team shipping.
- No abstract "AI" imagery: glowing waves, shields, servers, padlocks, icon diagrams.
- Images are full-bleed scenes. Type is printed into the scene, not on a window over a blank canvas.

## Motion
- No pulses or beat kicks. No breathing loops.
- Never fade a picture out and bring the same picture back.
- No blank frames at scene seams.
- A screen changes only with a visible cause (a click) and a page push.
- Captions: 10 to 15 short key lines, no word-by-word karaoke.

## Sound
- SFX are UI sounds only: clicks, soft whooshes, pops.

## Facts
- Pronunciation for TTS: write "Logiciel" as "Lawjishial".
- Every claim on screen must come from logiciel.io or the person asking.
